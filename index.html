Stud Finder

Find studs with sound, magnetism and AR — no extra hardware.

Try the demo No sensors needed: a simulated wall with hidden studs. Drag to look around, tap “Knock” to probe.
How it works
  • Knock acoustics. Drywall over a cavity vibrates like a drum skin: more energy below 400 Hz and a longer ring. Over a stud the panel is pinned, so the knock is shorter and brighter. Scores are relative to your other knocks, so the wall, your knuckle and the room cancel out.
  • Grid fitting. Studs sit at 16″ or 24″ centres. Instead of trusting any single knock, the app searches for the stud grid that best explains all of them, plus any clues you add (an outlet box is always nailed to a stud's side).
  • Sonar ranging. For correct AR scale the app measures your distance to the wall by playing a short chirp and timing its echo.
  • Magnetic screws. Steel screws hold the drywall to every stud. Slide the phone over them and the compass gets bent while the gyro doesn't. That disagreement is the signal.
  • AR overlay. The gyro tracks where the camera points, so knocks and studs stay pinned to the wall while you look around. Drag to re-align after you step sideways.

Use it as a guide, and confirm with a small nail or magnet before drilling. Avoid drilling near outlets, switches and plumbing.

Map the wall by knocking

  1. Stand an arm's length from the wall, phone held still in one hand.
  2. Put the crosshair on the wall and knock right there with a knuckle.
  3. Move the crosshair about 2″ (5 cm) sideways and knock again. Cover at least 3–4 ft (1 m+).
  4. Keep your knocks the same strength. Pivot the phone; don't walk.

Tip: open ⚙ and tap Measure with sonar first so the scale is right.

Starting…

How: lay the phone flat on the wall, screen facing you. Calibrate, then slide it slowly sideways without rotating it. Screws are spaced about 12″ (30 cm) apart up each stud, so sweep in a slow zig-zag. Where the reading spikes there's a screw, and the stud runs vertically through it. The magnetometer usually sits near the top of the phone.
Experimental. Press the phone's back firmly to the wall over a spot you know is hollow and calibrate. Then slide 1″ (2–3 cm) at a time, pausing for a probe at each spot. A sustained rise means the wall behaves differently there, which may be a stud. Works best in a quiet room. On Android the vibration motor adds a second channel.
